# Protection equipment guard

The shared protection-equipment bounds accept canonical equipment that grants
hexproof or shroud, optionally haste. The native facts exporter certifies the
whole card: one mana-only equip ability, unconditional attached keyword grants,
and no other effects, triggers, or replacements. This covers Lightning Greaves
without treating richer equipment as interchangeable.

In a quiet own precombat main phase, protection stays on a commander or reviewed
engine over a plain token with no haste benefit. Within either own main phase,
observed equipment hosts prevent repeated transfers back to an earlier host.

succeeded. An observed tap/untap, phase/turn change, or source leaving play resets
the relevant history. Fresh haste targets, unknown/modified states, tactical
windows, and explicit deck timing exceptions retain model discretion.
