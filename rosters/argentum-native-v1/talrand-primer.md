# Talrand, Sky Summoner — pilot primer

## Plan

Resolve Talrand with mana available, trade cheap interaction for opponents' highest
leverage plays, and turn every instant or sorcery into evasive pressure. Draw engines
and cost reduction let the deck play several spells per turn; token payoffs close the
game without tapping out early.

## Mulligan

Keep two or three lands, early mana, and at least one cheap draw or counterspell. A hand
with only reactive cards is acceptable only when it can still cast Talrand on schedule.

## Priorities

1. Counter effects that end the game or remove the engine at a large tempo gain.
2. Use cantrips before land drop when the next land is needed; otherwise keep mana open.
3. Sequence Talrand before low-value cantrips when it is safe.
4. Treat extra-turn and mass-bounce effects as closing tools, not routine value.

## Engine surfaces

Priority and stack decisions, counterspells, targeting, alternative timing, token
triggers, spell-cost reduction, graveyard casting, mass bounce, and extra turns.
