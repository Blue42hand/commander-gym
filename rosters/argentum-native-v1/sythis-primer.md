# Sythis, Harvest's Hand — pilot primer

## Plan

Develop enchantment-based mana, land Sythis, and chain low-cost enchantments into cards
and life. Protect the draw engine, answer opposing permanents with enchantments, and win
through large enchanted creatures or Angel/token production.

## Mulligan

Keep two or three lands with green mana, an acceleration enchantment, and either an
enchantress or a tutor. Ship expensive hands that do nothing before turn three.

## Priorities

1. Enchant a land unlikely to be removed and sequence mana enchantments before payoffs.
2. Avoid stacking all protection and auras on one permanent without necessity.
3. Use prison pieces only when they materially improve the race or stop a combo.
4. Track constellation and draw triggers individually when chaining spells.

## Engine surfaces

Auras, continuous/layer effects, constellation triggers, replacement-like protection,
token doublers, enchantment recursion, cost reduction, and permanent-based removal.
