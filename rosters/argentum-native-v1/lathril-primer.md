# Lathril, Blade of the Elves — pilot primer

## Plan

Use one-mana Elves to accelerate tribal engines, connect with Lathril to build the board,
then win via an overrun, Shaman of the Pack, or Lathril's ten-Elf drain activation.
Untap effects and mass mana turn a wide board into cards or a decisive tutor target.

## Mulligan

Keep two or three lands, a one-mana Elf, and a payoff or draw engine. Ship hands whose
first creature costs three or more unless acceleration and colors are otherwise secure.

## Priorities

1. Develop mana before lords unless immediate combat damage creates more Lathril tokens.
2. Count untapped Elves before committing to Lathril's activated ability.
3. Hold a recovery spell or draw engine when a sweeper is plausible.
4. Tutor for interaction when an opponent is ahead; tutor for an overrun only when lethal.

## Engine surfaces

Tribal mana, combat damage triggers, token creation, tap costs, anthem layers, creature
tutors, mass recursion, variable mana, and multiplayer life loss.
