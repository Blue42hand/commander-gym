# Krenko, Mob Boss — pilot primer

## Plan

Accelerate Krenko, give him haste or immediate activation access, then compound Goblin
count. Convert the board through combat, Impact Tremors, Goblin Bombardment, Pashalik
Mons, or a repeatable untap/mana loop.

## Mulligan

Keep two or three mana sources plus either acceleration and Krenko access, or a cheap
Goblin with a haste/untap enabler. Ship slow hands with no action before turn four.

## Priorities

1. Preserve Krenko until he can activate immediately.
2. Prefer Skullclamp or Idol of Oblivion when the hand otherwise runs out of material.
3. Count sacrifice mana and untap costs before entering a loop.
4. Do not expose every token to a likely sweeper when a smaller attack is lethal.

## Engine surfaces

Tokens, tribal continuous effects, sacrifice costs, mana abilities, copied activated
abilities, equipment, death/ETB triggers, extra combats, and multiplayer damage.
